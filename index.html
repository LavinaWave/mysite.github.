Free Zaznaika Historical Films Society

Our project to prepare schoolchildren for history Olympiads has been training prize‑winners and winners of Olympiads for ten years now. To celebrate our anniversary, we are launching a non‑profit project, Free Historical Films Society, featuring films dedicated to four important decades of the twentieth century. These films will help you become interested in the era and will help you spend time in a fun and useful way. At our paid seminars, among other things, we analyze the reliability and narrative of each of these films.

1910s: Marked by rapid industrial change and the upheaval of World War I (1914–1918). The decade ended with the 1918 influenza pandemic and major political shifts, including revolutions and redrawn borders.

1920s: Often called the “Roaring Twenties,” it saw economic growth, mass consumer culture, and the rise of jazz and cinema. It also featured social change—women’s suffrage in many countries—and ended with the 1929 stock market crash.

1930s: Dominated by the Great Depression, bringing widespread unemployment and economic hardship. Authoritarian regimes rose in Europe, while reforms like the New Deal reshaped government roles; escalating tensions set the stage for World War II.

1940s: Defined by World War II (1939–1945), the Holocaust, and unprecedented mobilization of science and industry, including the atomic bomb. The war’s end ushered in decolonization, the founding of the United Nations (1945), and the early Cold War.
